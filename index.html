How to play
  • Guess the secret word one letter at a time.
  • Click a letter tile, or type it on your keyboard.
  • Each wrong letter pops a balloon 🎈. Balloons are your lives.
  • Stuck? Tap 💡 Need a clue? for a fun hint.
  • Fill in every letter before the last balloon pops — or the monster gets a snack!
  • 2 players: Player 1 types a word, Player 2 guesses it.
